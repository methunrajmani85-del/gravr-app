Changelog

What changed in each version of GRAVR.

2.0November 2026

The biggest update yet: the logger, the coach, Food and settings were all rebuilt.

Logger

  • The set you are on is front and centre, with the done sets quiet beneath it.
  • A drop set is one set with its drops inside it.
  • Rest can start on its own or wait until you tap Start.
  • Target reps as a range, and a target effort for a set.
  • A coach drawer inside the logger, on the lift that is up.

Coach

  • The coach writes sets of every kind: working sets, warm-ups, drop sets, supersets, timed holds and effort targets.
  • It can add, change and remove upcoming sets, set rest, replace or reorder exercises, and rename a session.
  • Every change shows as a card that says exactly what will change. Apply it yourself, or let the coach make changes and undo any of them.
  • A Coach page in You that tests the AI service you connect, step by step, and shows a week of use.

Library and plans

  • Four ready-made plans under Library › Plans: beginner full body, calisthenics push, pull, legs, a no-equipment travel week, and a mobility routine.
  • 17 new movements, levers and planches among them, for 1,365 in all.
  • Plainer exercise names, and a short definition on every exercise page.

Food

  • A simpler page: what is left today, protein, carbs and fat against their targets, and today's meals.
  • Log again: your usual meals, one tap each.
  • Move between days from the top of the page.

Around the app

  • Six tabs, each with its name: Home, Data, Train, Food, Coach and You. Train sits in the centre.
  • You, in five groups: Athlete, Coach, Workout & logger, Data & privacy, About.
  • An intro that uses the real logger, and a short guide on every screen. Replay them from You.
  • Background patterns stay away from text.

Security and privacy

  • The key for an AI service is tied to the address it was saved with; a different address needs its own key.
  • Connections to an AI service stay on the host you chose, and replies are size-limited.
  • The app asks before the first send to any service, and says what will be sent.
1.0November 2026

The first version.

  • Log a set in a tap, add sets with one button, and swap, move or remove an exercise mid-session, with Undo.
  • Supersets, warm-ups and drop sets; timed sets for holds; distance and time for carries, rows and runs.
  • 1,348 animated exercises, found by the name on the machine.
  • A coach that answers from your log, on the iPhone or with an AI service you connect.
  • Food: type a meal, photograph it, or enter the numbers.
  • Apple Watch app with complications, and workouts saved to Apple Health.
  • Kilograms or pounds, with the right bar and plates for each.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the rest timer in the Dynamic Island, and Shortcuts.
  • iCloud backup, export, and Erase everything.
  • Made for iPhone Duo.
