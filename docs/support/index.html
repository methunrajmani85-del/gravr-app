Support

Help with GRAVR, the lifting and food log for iPhone.

Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/methunrajmani85-del/gravr-app/issues. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which iPhone and iOS version you use. Please do not post personal or health information.

Common questions

My workouts are not on my new iPhone. Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is on for GRAVR in Settings. Profile, then iCloud & backup, shows when the last backup ran.

How do I switch to pounds? Profile, then Training, then Weights in. Nothing in your log is changed.

How do I fix a set I typed wrong? Open the session from Data or History and tap Edit session.

The coach asks for an endpoint. The coach works on its own on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. To use another AI service, add its https address, model and your own key in Profile, then Coach.

How do I delete my data? Profile, then Erase everything. It removes your log from the iPhone and from your iCloud backup.
