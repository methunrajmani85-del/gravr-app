Support

Help with GRAVR, the lifting and food log for iPhone.

Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/methunrajmani85-del/gravr-app/issues. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which iPhone and iOS version you use. Please do not post personal or health information.

Common questions

My workouts are not on my new iPhone. Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is on for GRAVR in Settings. You, then Data & privacy, shows when the last backup ran.

How do I switch to pounds? You, then Athlete, then Weights in. Nothing in your log is changed.

How do I fix a set I typed wrong? Open the session from Data and tap Edit session.

Do I need to pay for AI? No. The coach works on its own on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, and everything else works with no AI at all. To have the coach change your plan and write your sets, add an AI service of your own in You, then Coach: its https address, model and your own key. That service bills you itself.

How do I see the intro or a guide again? Tap the ? at the top of a screen for its guide. The intro and the logger tour replay from You.

How do I delete my data? You, then Data & privacy, then Erase everything. It removes your log from the iPhone and from your iCloud backup.
