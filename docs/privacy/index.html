GRAVR privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

GRAVR is a workout and food log for iPhone. This page says what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and when anything leaves your iPhone.

The short version. GRAVR has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no server and never receives your data. What you log stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

What GRAVR stores

On your iPhone:

  • your workouts — sessions, exercises, sets, notes — and your weekly plan and saved workouts
  • your profile — height, bodyweight, year of birth, sex, goal — if you choose to enter it
  • your meals, and any photos of meals you take or pick
  • your conversations with the coach
  • your settings, and the address and model of a coach endpoint if you add one; its key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, GRAVR backs up your workouts, plan, saved workouts, profile and meals to your private iCloud database, so they come back on a new iPhone or after a reinstall. It is your iCloud storage, not ours: the developer cannot read it. Meal photos are not backed up; they stay on the iPhone that took them. Turn iCloud off for GRAVR in the Settings app and nothing is uploaded.

Apple Health

Only if you allow it:

  • GRAVR reads your bodyweight from Health, so you do not have to type it.
  • GRAVR writes your finished workouts, and an estimate of the energy they used, to Health.

A bodyweight read from Health is saved in your GRAVR profile like one you typed, so it is part of the iCloud backup above and of what the coach is sent below. GRAVR does not use Health data for advertising or marketing, and does not sell it or give it to data brokers. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app.

The coach

GRAVR's coach can run in three ways:

  • On your iPhone. With no endpoint set up, the coach uses the model built into iOS, where your iPhone supports it. Nothing leaves the device.
  • With an endpoint you add. You can connect an AI service of your own choosing by entering its address and your key for it. GRAVR asks before the first time anything is sent to it, and says what will be sent. With your agreement, a question to the coach is sent to that service together with what it needs to answer: your recent workout history, your profile numbers, and today's food. When you ask it to read a meal, the text you typed or the photos you chose are sent. That service handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer of GRAVR does not receive it.
  • Not at all. Everything else in the app works without the coach.

You can remove the endpoint in Profile at any time.

Camera and photos

GRAVR uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a meal. Photos are stored on your iPhone, and sent to your coach endpoint only when you ask it to read them.

Notifications and widgets

The rest timer sends a notification on your iPhone when a rest ends. Widgets and the Lock Screen timer show data from the app on your own device. None of this goes anywhere else.

What GRAVR does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

Your choices

  • Export. Profile → iCloud & backup exports your log as a file you keep.
  • Erase. Profile → Erase everything removes your workouts, plan, saved workouts, profile, meals and photos, coach conversations and endpoint from the iPhone, and from your iCloud backup.
  • Delete the app. Removing GRAVR removes what it stored on the iPhone.

Children

GRAVR is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact
